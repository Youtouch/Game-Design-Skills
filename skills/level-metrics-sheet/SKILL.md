# Level Metrics Sheet

Separate reachability, physical capability, visibility, inferred affordance, and subjective comfort. A conditional calculation can establish feasibility; it does not establish noticing, understanding, or enjoyment. Work at design level, without engine implementation.

## Choose the model

Identify the actual moving entity or view controls, ability stage, world state, viewpoint, sensory channels, and relevant constraints. For abstract or point-and-click spaces, model legal transitions and inspection actions rather than inventing an avatar. Keep missing dimensions symbolic or unknown. State units and scale.

Read [spatial reasoning](spatial-reasoning.md) for directed return paths, landmark handoffs, evidence order, route pacing, or deliberate disorientation. Read its [source notes](spatial-sources.md) when attributing a technique or precedent.

## Derive only relevant metrics

1. **Routes and returns.** Sketch directed connections with prerequisites, costs, one-way changes, and recovery/reset behavior. Trace the intended path, a detour, and consequences of irreversible actions. Check return or completion according to the brief. Enumerating a complete finite model supports stronger claims than sampling a few routes.
2. **Traversal.** Calculate transit with stated speed, stops, geometry, and state. Keep outbound and return costs separate. Distance divided by speed does not establish when travel feels empty; write that as an experience hypothesis.
3. **Capability.** Check relevant widths, heights, jumps, turns, or resources against the entity's capabilities. Identify omitted movement dynamics. A gap's feasibility and its apparent affordance are different claims; a deliberate misleading passage or future ability gate may deserve retention with clear stakes and recovery.
4. **Visibility and information.** Record viewpoint, occlusion, display scale, and available channels for navigation, observation, hazards, or combat. Check reverse views. Geometric exposure does not prove noticing or interpretation.
5. **Context-specific geometry.** Add cover, corridors, weapon ranges, or camera clearance only where relevant. Separate physical limits from provisional audience-specific challenge and comfort estimates; no universal safety margin follows from a maximum capability.

## Audit the level

Start with the route/state model before checking individual measurements. For each consequential claim, identify whether it concerns reachability, capability, visibility, inference, or comfort. Show the trace or calculation where possible; leave absent evidence explicit.

| Place / state | Claim type | Model / conditions | Trace or calculation | Cue and expected inference | Uncertainty / audience | Preserve or change | Smallest check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Recommend a repair or justified preservation against the brief. Include who benefits, the cost of misleading cues, and how interpretation can be revised or failure recovered. Intentional disorientation does not excuse an unrelated input barrier; an assist that reveals a conclusion differs from one that supplies equivalent evidence.

When observations conflict with a derived result, inspect model omissions, measurement error, build, ability stage, and participant context. Confusion alone does not overturn a correct conditional feasibility proof. A changed rule requires a new trace; an old observation does not validate the repair.
