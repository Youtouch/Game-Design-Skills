# Worked example: introducing the sprout seed in World 2

Builds on the [atomizer](../mechanic-atomizer/example.md) and [difficulty matrix](../difficulty-matrix/example.md) examples. Synthetic plan; beat numbers are illustrative.

**Scope:** World 2, four levels. **Audience:** finished World 1 (run, jump, wall-slide). **Brief:** by the end of World 2, players can plan a two-leaf chain under mild time pressure; optional rooms reward precision.

## 1. Prerequisites versus preferred order

| Concept | Real prerequisite? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Moss rule | yes, for all seed use | a throw at stone does nothing |
| Delay and lifetime | learned together | one forgiving crossing exposes both; a separate "delay only" beat adds length without removing a dependency |
| Seed supply (two, refill on ground) | yes, before chains | a chain fails silently without it |
| Throw from a leaf | yes, before chains | it is the chain |
| Wall-slide (World 1) | assumed | audience assumption; reminded in 2-3 because it last appeared in World 1 |

## 2–5. Plan

| Beat | Level | Mechanic(s) | Beat type | Band | New demand | Failure cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2-1 | seed pickup, moss rule | exposition | 1 | throw at a target | none: moss wall above a floor | the seed is found next to a single glowing moss wall; nothing else to throw at |
| 2 | 2-1 | leaf, delay, lifetime | exposition | 1 | wait for the leaf, then jump | none: 1 m drop to floor | leaf leads to a visible collectible, so the jump is invited, not ordered |
| 3 | 2-1 | moss rule | validation | 1 | choose moss among stone | short walk back | two surfaces, one moss; first required use |

| 8 | 2-3 | wall-slide | reminder | — | none new | floor below | last used in World 1 |

## 6. Transfer check

Trace bypasses: in 2-1, beat 4 could be skipped if the floor connects both sides. It does, on purpose, so completion of 2-1 does not show gap-crossing skill. Beat 5 in 2-2 has no floor under the critical gap, so it is the first point where completion implies the single-leaf skill. Beat 10 is the first fresh application of the chain without a safety floor.

## Prerequisite risks and justified exceptions

- **Risk:** beat 9 asks for throw-from-leaf and chain slack together. Isolating them would need a beat where the player throws from a leaf with nothing to chain to, which has no purpose in the fiction. Accepted, because the floor makes failure cheap. Watch for players who throw before landing on the leaf.
- **Exception:** beat 2 introduces delay and lifetime together (two new demands). Justified above.
- **Exception:** beat 11 is easier than beat 10. Intended: the climax is a reveal.

## Distinguishing observations

| Question | Observation |
|---|---|
| Did beat 3 teach the moss rule, or did players guess? | first-try choice in beat 3, then throws at non-moss in 2-2 |
| Is beat 9's difficulty slack, or ordering? | did falls happen before or after the second leaf opened? |
| Does the beat 6 secret feel like discovery or like a missed hint? | ask afterwards what players noticed in that room; do not prompt |
